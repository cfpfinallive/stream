The College Football Playoff (CFP) is an annual knockout invitational tournament to determine the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) champion. The CFP was established in 2014 to replace the controversial Bowl Championship Series (BCS). It expanded from four to 12 teams starting in the 2024 season. In 2026 College Football Playoff National Championship stands as a pinnacle event in NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) athletics, drawing millions of viewers and highlighting the sport's blend of tradition, competition, and innovation.
